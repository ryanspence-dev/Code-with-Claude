Liberal Democrats

Summary of the Liberal Democrats' 2024 General Election manifesto, "For A Fair Deal".

  • Introduce free personal care for people who need help with daily tasks, based on the Scottish model.
  • Guarantee access to a GP appointment within seven days, or 24 hours if urgent.
  • Recruit 8,000 more GPs and guarantee face-to-face appointments for those who want them.
  • Seek closer trade ties with the EU, starting with a new UK-EU customs union.
  • Abolish business rates and replace them with a {{ ref('commercial-landowner-levy', 'Commercial Landowner Levy') }}.
  • Raise the tax-free personal allowance when public finances allow.
  • Set a legally binding target to end sewage discharges into rivers, lakes, and coastal waters.
  • Target at least 90% of UK electricity from renewables by 2030.
  • Triple the {{ ref('early-years-pupil-premium', 'Early Years Pupil Premium') }} to £1,000 for disadvantaged pupils, and provide extra tutoring support.
  • Invest more in mental health support within schools.
  • Introduce proportional representation for Westminster elections.
  • Lower the voting age to 16.
