Plaid Cymru

Summary of Plaid Cymru's 2024 General Election manifesto, "For Fairness, For Ambition, For Wales".

  • Campaign for Welsh independence and greater devolution of powers to the Senedd.
  • Call for reform of the {{ ref('barnett-formula', 'Barnett formula') }} to secure fairer funding for Wales.
  • Secure the £4 billion owed to Wales from HS2 for transport and rail investment.
  • Establish a National Care Service for Wales, delivering social care free at the point of need.
  • Restore GP funding to 8.7% of the Welsh health budget and recruit 500 additional GPs.
  • Invest in flood defences and green energy jobs across Wales.
  • Support the target of one million Welsh speakers by 2050.
  • Protect and promote Welsh-medium education.
