Green Party

Summary of the Green Party of England and Wales' 2024 General Election manifesto, "Real Hope. Real Change."

  • Invest an average of £40 billion a year in a green economic transformation, including home insulation and heating upgrades, renewable electricity infrastructure, and transport and water infrastructure.
  • Restore nature through rewilding and expanded protected areas.
  • End new licences for fossil fuel extraction.
  • Introduce a wealth tax on assets above £10 million.
  • Increase capital gains tax so it matches income tax rates.
  • Equalise National Insurance contributions for higher earners.
  • Increase NHS dentistry funding to £3 billion a year by 2030, and roll out free dental nursing for children and low-income patients.
  • Introduce free personal care for older and disabled people, modelled on Scotland's existing scheme, backed by £20 billion a year of investment.
  • Build 150,000 new social rent homes a year.
  • Introduce rent controls to cap rises for tenants.
  • Scrap undergraduate tuition fees and restore maintenance grants.
  • Invest £5 billion in special educational needs (SEN) provision in mainstream schools.
