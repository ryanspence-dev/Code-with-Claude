Labour Party

Summary of the Labour Party's 2024 General Election manifesto, "Change".

  • Make "kickstarting economic growth" the party's first mission in government.
  • Commit to one major fiscal event (Budget) per year to give households and businesses stability.
  • Establish a National Wealth Fund to invest alongside the private sector in industry and infrastructure.
  • Deliver an extra 40,000 appointments each week, including evenings and weekends.
  • Cut NHS waiting times, meeting the existing 18-week treatment standard within five years.
  • Recruit thousands more NHS staff, partly funded by abolishing the non-domiciled tax status.
  • Set up Great British Energy, a publicly-owned clean energy company.
  • Aim for "clean power" - zero-carbon electricity generation - by 2030.
  • No new licences for oil and gas exploration in the North Sea (existing licences are unaffected).
  • Put 13,000 more neighbourhood police officers, PCSOs, and special constables into local communities.
  • Set up a Border Security Command to tackle people-smuggling gangs.
  • Introduce new powers to tackle anti-social behaviour.
  • Fund breakfast clubs in every primary school in England.
  • Recruit an additional 6,500 new teachers, funded by ending the VAT exemption for private schools.
  • Introduce a "New Deal for Working People", including day-one rights and a ban on exploitative zero-hours contracts.
  • Build 1.5 million new homes over the next parliament, with planning reform to speed up building.
