Conservative Party

Summary of the Conservative Party's 2024 General Election manifesto, "Clear Plan. Bold Action. Secure Future."

  • Cut employee National Insurance further, with an ambition to abolish it for self-employed workers over time.
  • Introduce a {{ ref('triple-lock-plus', '"triple lock plus"') }} so the tax-free pension allowance rises in line with the state pension.
  • Commit to reducing national debt as a share of the economy.
  • Increase NHS spending above inflation every year.
  • Guarantee a GP appointment within 7 days, or 24 hours if urgent, and recruit 8,000 more full-time-equivalent GPs.
  • Give a named GP to everyone aged over 70 and those with long-term conditions.
  • Introduce a binding annual cap on migration numbers, reducing them each year.
  • Proceed with the {{ ref('rwanda-scheme', 'Rwanda scheme') }} to deter small boat crossings.
  • Raise the earnings threshold required for family visas.
  • Introduce a form of mandatory National Service for 18-year-olds, offering military or civic/volunteering routes.
  • Increase defence spending to 2.5% of GDP by 2030.
  • Replace A-levels and T-levels over time with a new {{ ref('advanced-british-standard', '"Advanced British Standard"') }}.
  • Continue the rollout of free childcare hours for working parents.
  • Build 1.6 million homes over the next parliament.
  • Continue the {{ ref('mortgage-guarantee-scheme', 'Mortgage Guarantee Scheme') }} and introduce a new {{ ref('help-to-buy-scheme', 'Help to Buy equity loan scheme') }} for first-time buyers on new-build homes.
  • Abolish stamp duty for first-time buyers on homes up to £425,000.
