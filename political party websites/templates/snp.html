Scottish National Party (SNP)

Summary of the SNP's 2024 General Election manifesto, "A Future Made in Scotland".

  • Treat a majority of Scottish seats as a mandate to begin independence negotiations with the UK government.
  • Point to the mandate won at the 2021 Holyrood election as the basis for a second independence referendum.
  • Call for an increase in the UK minimum wage and an end to austerity spending cuts.
  • Call to scrap the two-child benefit cap and the associated "rape clause".
  • Back a Bill guaranteeing at least £16 billion a year for NHS England funding, which would generate extra funding for NHS Scotland via the Barnett formula.
  • Invest £300 million to help cut waiting times.
  • Support an independent Scotland re-joining the European Union.
  • Support a "just transition" for oil and gas workers into renewable energy jobs.
  • Oppose the building of new nuclear power stations in Scotland.
