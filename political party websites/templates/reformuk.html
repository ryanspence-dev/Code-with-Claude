Reform UK

Summary of Reform UK's 2024 General Election manifesto, "Our Contract with You".

  • Freeze non-essential immigration to ease pressure on housing, health, and infrastructure.
  • Leave the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR) to allow faster deportation of illegal immigrants.
  • Introduce a strict points-based work visa system, including higher employer National Insurance contributions for foreign workers.
  • Raise the income tax personal allowance to £20,000.
  • Raise the higher-rate tax threshold to £70,000.
  • Abolish IR35 rules for the self-employed.
  • Offer tax relief on private healthcare and insurance to reduce pressure on the NHS.
  • Cut management consultancy spending and reduce NHS waste.
  • Scrap the 2050 net zero target.
  • Fast-track new licences for North Sea oil and gas.
  • Scrap net zero subsidies, including for wind, solar, and low-carbon farming schemes.
  • Introduce a "patriotic curriculum" with balanced coverage of topics such as empire and slavery.
  • Ban the teaching of transgender ideology in schools, including social transitioning and pronoun swapping.
  • Replace the House of Lords with a smaller, democratically elected chamber.
  • Hold a referendum on introducing proportional representation for the House of Commons.
